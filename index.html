The Legend

Chapter I

The Massacre of the Century

For centuries the ruined continent has known a single law: the survival of the most ruthless. Lands that once prospered are desolate wastes now, crawling with twisted beasts and ruled by the brute force of profane sorcery.

Every hundred years a silent, maddening call echoes across the land, dragging the most powerful and most desperate creatures alive to the cyclopean gates of the Ancient Capital. This is the dawn of the King's Tournament. No alliances. No rules. No mercy. A brutal convergence where outcasts, ancient entities and masters of the arcane collide in absolute carnage.

Chapter II

The Throne and the Void

The last mage standing wins more than a crown of bone. By proving mind and body unbreakable, the survivor claims absolute dominion over the continent for the next hundred years. But political power is the smallest prize on the table.

The real one waits in the abyssal catacombs beneath the castle: the mythical Elixir of the King. Fragmented accounts and forgotten grimoires whisper that the chalice holds no mundane potion, but the liquid essence of the void itself. Whoever drinks it has their mortal limits shattered — an endless well of magic, and an ascension to something close to a god walking among the ruins.

Chapter III

The Living Arena

The castle that hosts the tournament is no backdrop of dead stone. It is alive, aware, and hungry.

As the massacre unfolds, the Magic Ruin — a cosmic, suffocating mist — begins to swallow the outer reaches of the fortress. The anomaly dissolves the sanity and the flesh of anything caught inside it, driving the mages further and further toward the central courtyard. Hiding is not an option. The castle forces the confrontation, squeezing the competitors until one spell prevails.
